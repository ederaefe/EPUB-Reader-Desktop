SEMPLY EBOOK READER · USER MANUAL · FREE & OFFLINE

Read deeply, without clutter or paywalls.

Semply Ebook Reader is a calm, native desktop reader for Windows, macOS, and Linux. Open EPUB, PDF, MOBI, and TXT books locally, reflow dense PDFs offline, annotate in six theme-aware styles, and keep your entire library under your own control.

Every surface in Semply is tuned for long-form focus. Switch between single-page and two-page spreads, highlight passages that stay crisp on both dark and light paper, and press F11 whenever you want every bar to disappear.

Getting Started

Semply Ebook Reader runs completely locally on your computer with zero accounts, zero tracking nags, and zero PRO paywalls. Every book, highlight, bookmark, and shelf is saved directly on your device.

Supported Formats

EPUB (EPUB 2 & 3), PDF (native HiDPI view plus offline Reflow Mode), MOBI, and plain TXT documents.

Supported Platforms

Installers are packaged for Windows (NSIS x64), macOS (DMG & ZIP), and Linux (AppImage & DEB).

Your First Three Actions

  1. Add books to your shelf: Click the dashed + Add book card in the first library slot, press Ctrl + O, or drag and drop files directly anywhere onto the Semply window.
  2. Open a book from your OS: Double-click an .epub or .pdf file associated with Semply to import and open it in the existing window immediately.
  3. Pick up where you left off: Use the bottom Continue reading bar in the Library to jump straight back to your last page.
Offline by default Reading, cover extraction, full-book search, notes, bookmarks, PDF Reflow, Read Aloud, and JSON/ZIP backups all work with no internet connection. Network requests happen only when you click online Dictionary, Wikipedia, or Translate actions.

Library & Importing Books

Your Library gives you a clean, fast catalog with both Grid View (book cover tiles with progress overlays) and List View (compact rows showing title, author, date added, note count, and reading progress).

Ways to Import Books

  • Single or Multiple Files: Click the + Add book card (or press Ctrl + O) and pick Add Book to select one or more files.
  • Entire Folder Scan: Choose Add Folder to recursively scan nested folders and import every supported .epub, .pdf, .mobi, and .txt book automatically.
  • Full-Window Drag & Drop: Drag files from File Explorer or Finder straight onto Semply.
  • Bulk Add to a Custom Category: Open any custom category in the left sidebar and click the + card to pick existing books from your main library in bulk.

Automatic Covers & Metadata Cleanup

When you import a book, Semply automatically reads embedded cover art and metadata, strips noisy download prefixes from filenames, and renders a crisp miniature first-page preview when a book has no cover image. Right-click any book card and select Refresh Cover / First Page if you ever want to regenerate its thumbnail.

Shelves, Sorting & Right-Click Actions

  • Built-in Shelves: Filter quickly by All, Recently Read, Favorites, Formats (EPUB or PDF), Read, and Deleted (soft-delete trash with one-click Restore).
  • Custom Color Categories: Click + Add new category in the sidebar, enter a name, and pick from 9 preset swatches or any custom hex color.
  • 5-Column Sorting: Click Title, Author, Date, Notes, or Progress to toggle ascending or descending order.
  • Book Context Menu: Right-click any book to Open, Favorite, Mark Read/Unread, assign a category, Refresh Cover, Convert PDF to EPUB (Offline), Reveal in Explorer, or Move to Trash.

Reading, Themes & Fullscreen

The reader toolbar stays intentionally quiet: navigation panels (Library, Contents, Notes, Bookmarks, and Find) sit on the left, while Immersive Fullscreen and Settings sit on the right.

3-Tab Reader Settings Panel

Click the gear button on the top right to open the floating settings panel, organized into three zero-scroll tabs:

  • Display: Switch themes (Light, Paper, Dusk, Obsidian), pick a typeface (Publisher, Sans, Serif, Mono), adjust font size, pick Tight / Normal / Relaxed line spacing, toggle Single / Double page spread, and tune screen dimming or the Warm Night Filter.
  • Flow: Control Auto-Scroll (Fixed Timer or Smart Pace) and Read Aloud playback speed and pause/mute.
  • PDF / More: Switch between Original PDF and Reflow EPUB (when viewing a PDF), export a PDF as an offline EPUB, or jump to All Preferences and Keyboard Shortcuts.

Distraction-Free Fullscreen & Quiet Progress

  • True Immersive Fullscreen (F11): Hides the titlebar, top toolbar, footer, side arrows, and drawers so only your book text fills the screen. Hover the top-right corner or press Esc or F11 to exit.
  • Dual-State Progress Bar: At rest, the bottom edge displays five subtle dots. Move your cursor over the bottom strip to reveal the exact page counter and a full-width page scrubber.
  • Content-Only Zoom: Press Ctrl + +, Ctrl + -, or Ctrl + 0 to scale your book text size (or PDF page zoom) cleanly without distorting window buttons or toolbars.

Native PDF & Offline Reflow

PDFs open natively in a crisp, HiDPI canvas viewer with selectable text, single or dual-page spreads, 50%–300% zoom, native PDF outlines in the Contents drawer, bookmarks, and automatic Night and Sepia color adaptation.

One-Click Offline PDF Reflow Mode

Reading a fixed-layout PDF on a laptop or in dark mode can feel cramped. Open Settings → PDF and select Reflow to transform the PDF into a responsive EPUB completely offline using Artifex MuPDF:

  • Automatically removes repeating running headers, footers, and page numbers.
  • Preserves headings, bold/italic emphasis, monospace code blocks, chapter bookmarks, and embedded illustrations.
  • Safely preserves scanned pages or full-page diagrams as crisp responsive images so nothing is lost.
  • Caches the reflowed EPUB locally so reopening Reflow Mode later is instantaneous.
  • Unlocks every EPUB tool on your PDF: custom fonts, sizing, highlights, notes, full-book Find, Read Aloud, and Auto-Scroll.

Want a permanent copy? Click Export as EPUB (Offline) inside Settings, or right-click any PDF in your Library and select Convert to EPUB (Offline) to add the converted EPUB directly to your shelf.

Editing, Organizing & Exporting Notes

  • Click any highlight on the page: Switch its color live, add or edit a comment (Ctrl + Enter to save), copy the quote and note together, read it aloud, or delete it with an instant Undo toast.
  • Notes Drawer: Filter by color or commented-only notes, search across your annotations, sort by Page Order / Newest / Oldest / Color, or toggle Group by Chapter.
  • Multi-Format Export: Export a book's highlights and notes via the native Save dialog as a styled standalone HTML report, Markdown (.md), or Plain Text.

Smart Bookmarks (Ctrl + B)

Press Ctrl + B (or click the top-right bookmark ribbon) to bookmark the current spread. Semply automatically captures the opening sentence of that page as an excerpt so you can recognize each bookmark inside the Bookmarks drawer, where you can rename, search, sort, or jump straight back.

Full-Book Search (Ctrl+F)

Press Ctrl + F (or click the magnifying glass in the reader toolbar) from anywhere — whether focus is on the window or inside the book text — to open the left-docked Find in Book panel.

  • Searches every chapter across the entire EPUB automatically as you type (2+ characters).
  • Displays each result with its chapter name and an inline amber-highlighted excerpt.
  • Press Enter to step to the next match or Shift + Enter for the previous match; each active match is temporarily highlighted on the page and cleaned up automatically when you close Find.
  • Active Auto-Scroll pauses automatically while the Find panel is open and resumes when you dismiss it with Esc.

Auto-Scroll (Fixed & Smart)

Enable hands-free page turning from Settings → Flow (or All Preferences → Reading settings). Both modes accept an exact manual duration from 3 to 600 seconds.

Fixed Timer

Turns the page on a strict, predictable countdown (default 20s). Ideal for steady skimming or timed reading drills.

Smart Pace

Scales your manual base time (default 25s for a typical 180-word spread) to the actual number of words currently visible on screen, with optional 300ms micro-pauses at chapter dividers and blockquotes.

Hands-Free Safeguards

  • Quiet Bottom-Right Countdown: Shows remaining seconds unobtrusively even in Immersive Fullscreen. Click the number anytime to pause, change modes, or adjust seconds.
  • Wheel Nudges (Smart Mode): Scroll up slightly to slow your pace by 15%, or scroll down to speed it up by 10%.
  • Automatic Suspension: Pauses cleanly whenever you open a drawer, hover or read a footnote, select text, or switch away from Semply — and automatically locks into a safe pause if you leave the app for 5+ minutes.

Read Aloud, Dictionary & Footnotes

Read Aloud (Text-to-Speech)

Start Read Aloud from Settings → Flow, or highlight any passage and click Speak. Semply normalizes common book text before speaking — expanding abbreviations (Dr., e.g., etc.), decimals (3.14 → "3 point 14"), dotted acronyms, and Roman numeral headings (Chapter IV → "Chapter 4"). Adjust speed (0.8x–2.0x), pitch, and system voice in All Preferences → Text to speech.

Inline Dictionary, Wikipedia & Translation

Select any word or phrase to see instant phonetics and definitions (via Free Dictionary API) or concise encyclopedic summaries (via Wikipedia) inside a floating card, or launch deep lookups in Merriam-Webster, Oxford Learners, Cambridge, Collins, Vocabulary.com, Wiktionary, Google Translate, or DeepL.

Non-Destructive Footnotes

Clicking superscript footnote links or endnote references opens a compact bottom preview card — even when the footnote lives in a separate endnotes chapter — so you never lose your page. Click Jump to note if you want to navigate there directly.

Warm Night Filter & Dimming

For late-night reading, open Settings → Display (or All Preferences → Reading settings) to combine two hardware-independent eye-comfort layers:

  • Screen Dimming: Smoothly lowers viewport brightness without changing your operating system display settings.
  • Warm Blue Light Night Filter: Applies a calibrated warm amber tint from candlelight 2000K to warm neutral 4500K with adjustable intensity across both EPUB and PDF views.

Backup & Additive Sync

Open All Preferences → Backup & Sync (via the Sidebar Settings icon or Reader Settings → More) to export or merge your library safely across computers.

  • Export JSON: Lightweight snapshot of all book metadata, reading progress, custom shelves, highlights, notes, bookmarks, and preferences.
  • Export ZIP Archive: Full portable bundle including library_metadata.json plus every book's extracted cover art.
  • Non-Destructive Additive Import: Importing a backup never wipes your local data. Semply unions your custom categories, deduplicates books by path or title/author, restores missing covers from ZIP archives, advances reading progress only when the backup is further along, and appends new highlights and bookmarks without duplicates.

Troubleshooting & FAQ

Does Semply Ebook Reader require an internet connection or account?

No. Semply works 100% offline with no login, telemetry lock-in, or paywall. Everything stays in your local IndexedDB database and disk folders. Internet access is used only when you click online Dictionary, Wikipedia, or Translate lookups.

Why does a book show a miniature first page instead of cover art?

Some EPUBs and plain TXT files do not bundle an embedded cover image. Rather than displaying a blank placeholder, Semply typesets the book's real opening page onto a warm-paper miniature thumbnail. For PDFs, Semply renders Page 1 automatically.

Why didn't Ctrl+R reload or reset my reading position?

By design. Semply blocks accidental browser shortcuts like Ctrl+R, F5, Ctrl+W, and Alt+Left/Right so you never accidentally lose your place or drop back to the Library while reading.

How do I open a book's original file location on disk?

Right-click any book card in your Library and choose Reveal in Explorer (or Finder on macOS) to highlight the source file directly in your operating system's file manager.

Can I read a scanned PDF in Reflow Mode?

Yes. When Semply's offline MuPDF engine detects a scanned or diagram-heavy page with fewer than 25 extractable text characters, it preserves that page as a crisp responsive image inside the reflowed EPUB so no visual information is lost.
