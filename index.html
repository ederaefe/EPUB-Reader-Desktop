The Features That Set Us Apart

We stripped away the clunky menus of legacy apps and built a modern reading engine focused on intelligent UI, automation, and speed.

An interface that breathes.

Immerse yourself in a distraction-free, beautifully minimalist reading environment. Semply’s intelligent rendering engine dynamically adapts margins, contrast, and typography to your screen—giving you a flawless, book-like feel that simply gets out of your way.

Intelligent Rendering • Modern UI

Read completely hands-free.

Put your feet up. Set a fixed timer, or let our Smart Pace algorithm automatically adapt to the text density on your screen—speeding up for sparse dialogue and slowing down for dense prose.

Adaptive Smart Pace

Curate your library, instantly.

Fix missing covers, authors, and series data right from your library view. No need to open clunky external editors—just click, type, and organize your collection flawlessly in seconds.

Frictionless In-App Editing

Your knowledge, unleashed.

Stop letting walled gardens trap your highlights. Semply exports your notes and annotations in clean Markdown and JSON, ready to drop seamlessly into Obsidian, Notion, or your personal knowledge base.

Universal Note Export

Zero lag. Zero indexing screens.

Built on raw native desktop architecture. Search across a 500-page book in milliseconds, open massive PDFs instantly, and glide through huge libraries without the spinning beachballs.

Native Desktop Speed

Your library. Your files. Your rules.

We don't believe in DRM lock-in or cloud subscriptions. Semply is a local-first haven for your EPUBs and PDFs. You own your files, your reading progress, and your data—forever. No telemetry. No tracking. Just pure reading.

100% Local-First • Privacy Focused
