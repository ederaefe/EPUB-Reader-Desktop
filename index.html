The Core Principle

Your Books Belong to You, Not to a Corporation

Modern reading apps turn your books into rented cloud licenses and recurring bills. Semply is built on digital sovereignty: your books stay on your computer, forever under your own control.

100% Private on Disk

Your Library Stays On Your Computer

Your books, shelves, notes, and reading progress reside safely on your machine. Nothing is ever uploaded to distant corporate servers or commercial databases.

  • Storage: Private folder on your device
  • Privacy: Zero analytics, zero tracking
  • Security: Your files never leave your control
Always Accessible

Read 100% Offline Forever

Open your library on flights, off-grid cabins, and daily train commutes. Semply never requires an active internet connection or login check to enjoy your books.

  • Connection: Zero internet required to read
  • Continuity: Works anywhere, anytime
  • Reliability: Immune to cloud server outages

Free from Subscriptions & Accounts

Thoughtful Details

Crafted for People Who Love Books

Every feature in Semply solves an everyday frustration that avid readers face with traditional reading apps.

In-Place Footnotes: Never Lose Your Page

Clicking a footnote reference in traditional readers jolts you hundreds of pages away to an appendix at the back of the book. In Semply, footnotes open in a gentle, in-place popover right where you are reading. Read the citation, close it, and continue smoothly without ever losing your line.

Non-Destructive Reading • Zero Disorientation

Smart Pace Hands-Free Reading

Read comfortably without touching the mouse or keyboard. Smart Pace measures the visible word count on your screen to pace page turns naturally, and injects thoughtful micro-pauses at chapter dividers.

Adapts to Page Density

Reflowable PDF Reading

Reading rigid PDF files on laptop screens used to mean tiny text and endless horizontal panning. Semply transforms static PDFs into comfortable, resizable text that flows naturally like a real book.

Clean Text Reflow

Candlelight Night Filter & Eye Care

Protect your eyes during late-night bedtime sessions. A warm amber filter softens harsh blue light, and gentle screen dimming keeps reading comfortable in dark rooms.

Bedtime Eye Comfort

Over 70 Classic Book Fonts

Enjoy typography curated specifically for sustained reading, including classic book serifs like Baskerville and Georgia, alongside dyslexia-friendly accessible typefaces.

Timeless Typography

Whisper-Quiet Battery Efficiency

Designed lean and fast. Semply uses negligible memory and stays completely silent between page turns, letting you read for hours on laptop battery without heating up your machine or spinning fans.
