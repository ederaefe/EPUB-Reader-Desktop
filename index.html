Privacy First

Your Books and Data, Always Under Your Control

Semply is built for complete privacy. We believe your reading habits, notes, and library are yours alone, and our design reflects that.

Simplicity

No Accounts Required

You don’t need an account or internet connection to use Semply. It’s ready to read the moment you open it, ensuring your experience stays private and distraction-free.

Transparency

Open Source

We operate with full transparency. Because Semply is open source, anyone can inspect the code to verify that we stay true to our design.

Engineered for Focus

Engineered Beyond Conventional Readers

Semply bridges native desktop horsepower with thoughtful typography, distraction-free ergonomics, and algorithmic reading automation.

Beautifully Readable PDFs

Stop squinting at rigid, multi-column PDFs. Semply automatically transforms them into fluid, readable text that adapts to your screen, font, and preferences—all done instantly on your machine without cloud uploads.

Works Offline • No Cloud Needed

Dual-Mode Hands-Free Auto-Scroll

Read hands-free with precision. Choose between Fixed Countdown Timer (3 to 600s) or Smart Pace, which dynamically counts visible word density on each page to calculate the ideal reading interval automatically.

Fixed Seconds & Smart Pace WPM

77-Font Curated Typography Library

Includes 12 built-in publisher fonts plus 65 curated Google Fonts spanning Book Serif, Clean Sans, Editorial Slab, and OpenDyslexic accessibility faces. Downloaded once, cached to disk forever.

Offline-First Caching Engine

Dual-Anchor SVG Annotations

Highlights never drift. Semply anchors annotations by both exact text substring and DOM CFI ranges, rendering SVG overlays that survive font size adjustments, margin widening, and window resizes.

Drift-Proof Highlights & Export

Four Contrast-Preserving Themes

Obsidian (#0a0a0a), Clean Daylight, Warm Paper Sepia, and Dusk Evening. Carefully engineered semantic CSS tokens prevent image contrast inversions while synchronizing native OS window backdrops.

Global F9 Instant Cycling

Instant Full-Book Spine Chapter Search

Search multi-hundred-page volumes in milliseconds. Semply iterates across the entire EPUB spine in the background, extracting surrounding context sentences with regex matching and instant one-click jump navigation without blocking your reading thread.

Background Spine Indexing • Zero UI Lag

# 3. Launch live developer build

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about Semply's security, supported formats, and offline capabilities.

Semply supports reflowable EPUB (.epub) files and native PDF (.pdf) documents. PDFs can be viewed as they are or automatically transformed into clean, resizable paragraphs so they read perfectly on your screen.

Semply connects to external servers for exactly two transparent reasons: (1) checking for signed application updates, and (2) downloading fonts directly when you explicitly choose a new one. Once downloaded, fonts are cached to disk and work 100% offline forever.
In Smart Pace mode, Semply automatically adjusts the auto-scroll speed based on how much text is on the page. Dense pages move slower, while sparse chapters move faster, ensuring a natural reading flow.
