Thoughtful Details

Crafted for People Who Love Books

Every feature in Semply solves an everyday frustration that avid readers face with traditional reading apps.

Offline PDF Reflow: Turn Rigid Documents into Living Books

Reading static, two-column PDFs on laptop screens has always meant tiny fonts, clumsy zooming, and endless horizontal panning. Semply reconstructs complex PDF documents into flowing, resizable book chapters on your local machine—freeing your text so you can change font sizes, pick your favorite typeface, and read comfortably without uploading sensitive files to cloud servers.

100% Offline Reflow • Zero Cloud Uploads • Resizable Flow

Floating Notes & Thought Capture

Select any sentence to spawn a sleek, floating toolbar. Highlight passages in soft paper hues and record thoughts in anchored cards that never obscure your book. Export your notes in one click to Obsidian, Notion, or plain text.

Anchored Inline Notes • 1-Click Export

One-Click Portable Library Backups

Back up your entire collection, notes, and reading progress into a single portable file. Move to a new laptop and restore your personal sanctuary in seconds without losing a single bookmark.

Portable Archive • Zero Lock-In

In-Place Footnote Peek

Never get jolted to the back of the book again. Tap any citation reference to read the note in a gentle popover right above the text, close it, and continue your reading flow without losing your place.

Non-Destructive Reading • Zero Disorientation

Whisper-Quiet Battery Efficiency

Built lean and fast with zero background sync daemons or telemetry loops. Semply launches in milliseconds and stays silent between page turns, letting you read for hours on battery without warming your laptop.

Hours of Reading on Battery

In-App Book Browser: Curated Free Libraries & Instant Ingestion
